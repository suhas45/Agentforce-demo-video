Duplicate checks, lead entry and conversion ignore the email.

Some customers share one email address between several people, like a QC team that uses a single inbox.
Today, Salesforce blocks the second contact as a duplicate. And lead conversion can link a lead to the wrong person, because it matches on email.
So, Conscendo added a Shared / Generic Email flag on Lead and Contact.
When it's ticked, duplicate checks and conversion ignore the email, and match people by mobile number and name instead.
