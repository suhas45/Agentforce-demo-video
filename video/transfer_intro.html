C Fashion is a fashion retail company, with stores and distribution centers across the country.
But when a customer wants a product that's out of stock in the store, finding it somewhere else can be slow and manual.
So, Conscendo built the Store Transfer Agent with Agentforce, right inside Slack, where store teams already work.
The agent uses real-time inventory in Salesforce to find stock nearby, and creates transfers and delivery orders in seconds.
